<!-- feature-intro -->
Asset Count shows which downloadable assets people actually use. Send visitors through a tracked asset URL, keep the original file delivery intact, and bring the resulting totals back into Craft.
<!-- feature-intro-end -->

<!-- feature-section -->
## Track every download

Generate a tracked URL for any Craft asset and use it wherever you offer a download. Each visit is counted before the visitor is redirected to the original file, so your templates stay in control of the surrounding experience.

<!-- feature-section-end -->

<!-- feature-grid -->
- :icon[chart-bar] **Visible totals** Show download counts in Craft or retrieve them in Twig alongside the asset.
- :icon[refresh] **Reset actions** Reset an asset’s count from the control panel when a campaign or reporting period changes.
- :icon[settings] **Configurable behaviour** Manage the counting rules in plugin settings or project configuration.
<!-- feature-grid-end -->

<!-- feature-section -->
## Cleaner download counts

Exclude known IP addresses, signed-in users, or likely bots when those requests should not affect the total.
<!-- feature-section-end -->
